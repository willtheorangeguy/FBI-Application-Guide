# FBI Application Guide — FAQ

## Is this official?

No. It is an unofficial personal compilation of publicly available federal material. It is not
produced, endorsed, reviewed, or approved by the FBI or any US government agency.

The authoritative source is [fbijobs.gov](https://fbijobs.gov/).

## How current is it?

Unknown, and that is the honest answer. Nothing records when each document was collected or
which version it came from, and nothing updates automatically.

Requirements, testing standards, and fitness scoring all change. Confirm anything you intend
to act on against the official source.

## Can I rely on this for my application?

Use it as an orientation aid — a map of the stages and what each involves. Verify specifics
against the agency before acting on them.

## Why is every document in two formats?

`.docx` so you can fill in worksheets, adapt the resume guide, and annotate; `.pdf` for
reading and sharing with a fixed layout. Step 3's citizenship worksheets and the Federal
Resume Guide are meant to be completed, so the editable copy is the point.

## Why does "Testing Overview" appear three times?

Steps 2, 5, and 6 each cover a different testing phase. They are not copies of one another.

## Who wrote the documents?

The originating agencies — the FBI, FBIJobs, and USCIS. This repository arranges and
republishes them; it does not author them.

## What licence applies?

The compilation and any original material are MIT. The documents themselves originated with
federal agencies, so the content licence in the repository does not straightforwardly apply
to them — this needs review. See [`internal/known-issues.md`](./internal/known-issues.md).

## Does it include a firearms manual?

No. The feature list previously claimed a Glock 19 and 22 user manual; no such document is in
the repository.

## Can I contribute?

Yes — corrections and newer versions of source documents are useful, especially with a note of
where and when they were obtained. See the org contributing guide.

## Where do I start?

`0 - Title and Contents`, then the folder for the stage you are at. See [Usage](./usage.md).
